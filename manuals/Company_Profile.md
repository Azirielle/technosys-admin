### III. SERVICES OFFERED
a) Supply and installation of Window Type, Split Type, Ducted, Chilled Water and VRF Air conditioning units.
b) Supply and installation of ventilation and exhaust systems.
c) Supply and installation of refrigerant units.
d) Fabrication and installation of air ducts.

f) Repair of refrigeration systems.
g) Repair of centrifugal, reciprocating and screw compressors.
h) Service and maintenance of air conditioning and refrigeration units.
